About the Forest

Spanning over 5,000 hectares, Kersik Luway is a protected nature reserve famous for its unique sandy soil and being the primary habitat of the rare Coelogyne pandurata, the Black Orchid. This ecological wonder offers a surreal landscape where white silica sands meet dense tropical vegetation.

Visitors can walk along guided paths to witness the blooming season and diverse epiphytic plants. Beyond the famed black orchid, the reserve is home to over 50 species of orchids and various endemic wildlife of Borneo, making it a sanctuary for botanists and nature lovers alike.
